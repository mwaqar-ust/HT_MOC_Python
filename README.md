**Breif Overview of Repository**

This repository contains computational algorithms to simulate hydraulic transient (water-hammer) waves in simple pipeline systems with various forms of damping such as steady friction, unsteady friction and pipe wall visco-elaaticity.

**References**

To learn more about the mathematical background, please read the following classical books and publications:
1. Wylie, E. B., Streeter, V. L., & Suo, L. (1993). Fluid transients in systems (Vol. 1, p. 464). Englewood Cliffs, NJ: Prentice Hall.
2. Chaudhry, M. H. (2014). Applied Hydraulic Transients. 3rd Edition, Springer publications.
3. Ghidaoui, M. S., Zhao, M., McInnis, D. A., & Axworthy, D. H. (2005). A review of water hammer theory and practice. Appl. Mech. Rev., 58(1), 49-76.
